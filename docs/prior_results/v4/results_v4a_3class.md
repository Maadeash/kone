## G. Combined class -- reported separately as a limitation

Only three physical bearings (KB23, KB24, KB27) carry the combined inner+outer fault. Under leave-bearing-out, at most two are ever available for training and at most one for testing in any fold. Some folds contain no combined test bearing at all (see fold table above), so per-fold combined F1 is undefined rather than zero in those folds.

| Model | Variant | Combined F1 (mean +/- std, 4-class model) |
|---|---|---|
| RF | `raw` | 0.000 +/- 0.000 |
| MLP | `raw` | 0.198 +/- 0.389 |
| LR | `raw` | 0.001 +/- 0.002 |

This is a data-availability ceiling, not a modelling failure. It is not fixable by architecture or feature changes; it needs more combined-fault specimens.
