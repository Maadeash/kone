### `healthy`: NOT MEASURABLE

> All three healthy recordings are from 2022-01-25 (3/0/0 across the three acquisition days), so healthy-vs-faulty is perfectly confounded with acquisition session. Under leave-one-session-out, holding out 2022-01-25 leaves training with no healthy recordings, and holding out either other day leaves the test set with none. No session-aware protocol on this dataset can evaluate it. See docs/data_notes_d2.md 7D.

No healthy number is reported here, on any metric card, or as a fusion input. The confound is total rather than partial, so a caveated number would be worse than none.
